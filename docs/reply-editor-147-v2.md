> One more point on the self-coupling term, for the editor and the
> reviewers. The ReScience C rules ask that a replication "be based
> exclusively on the originally published paper, although exceptions are
> admitted if properly documented in the replication article" (Rougier et
> al. 2017, PeerJ Comput. Sci. 3:e142, doi:10.7717/peerj-cs.142, p. 4).
> NH-01 sets $J_{ii} = 0$ in every arm, a convention taken from the
> 2024-2025 algorithms of the original group, not from the 2019 article,
> whose field (Eq. 2.16) keeps the diagonal. We declare this as a
> documented exception to that rule. The draft v2 (PR #8 of
> hypneum-lab/rescience-nh01, not yet frozen) states it in deviation 2 and
> gives its measured effect from the post-freeze ablation at $t = 10$:
> dream/Hebb ratio 3.90 with the diagonal zeroed in both arms, 1.90 with
> it kept in both arms, 3.05 for the mixed ratio (kept-diagonal dream over
> zero-diagonal Hebb). The frozen package is unchanged.

Sources checked on 2026-11-04/05: Rougier et al. 2017 p. 4 (PDF in the
lab library, `references/doi/10.7717_peerj-cs.142.pdf`); the three ratios
are those of the v2 draft, deviation 2.
